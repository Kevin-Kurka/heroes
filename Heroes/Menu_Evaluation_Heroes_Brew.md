# American Heroes & Brew — Full Menu Evaluation

**Date:** March 22, 2026
**Location:** 300 Carlsbad Village Dr, Suite 101, Carlsbad CA 92008
**Benchmark:** Draft Republic Carlsbad, Park 101, Carlsbad Village average

---

## 1. OVERALL ASSESSMENT

**Grade: B-** — Strong identity (Philly, burgers, American theme), solid breakfast program, good daily specials. But the menu has pricing gaps, redundancies, missing high-margin categories, and several items that are likely underperforming. There's $3-5 per ticket left on the table.

---

## 2. WHAT'S WORKING

- **The Philly** — An Amoroso roll cheesesteak in Carlsbad is a genuine differentiator. Nobody else does this. Keep it, protect it, feature it more prominently.
- **Burger variety at $16 base** — 8 named burgers at $16 is aggressive vs. Draft Republic ($20 with beer). This is a traffic driver. The city-name theming is strong branding.
- **Breakfast program** — Neither Park 101 nor Draft Republic serves breakfast. You own mornings in the sports bar category. The breakfast burrito lineup with escalating protein pricing is smart.
- **Daily Lineup specials** — Every day has a deal. This drives repeat visits and gives regulars a reason to come on slow days.
- **Shelter to Soldier donation on cheesecake** — Great community tie-in. On-brand for "Heroes."
- **Bottomless Mimosas at $25** — Competitive. Draft Republic charges similar. Drives weekend brunch traffic.

### 3C. Protein Add-On Pricing is Inconsistent

Your protein upcharges vary wildly across the menu:

| Protein | Nachos | Burrito | Tacos |
|---------|--------|---------|-------|
| Chicken | +$8 | $16 | N/A |
| Carnitas | +$6 | $17 | Included |
| Carne Asada | +$10 | $18 | Included |
| Pastrami | N/A | $18 | N/A |

**Problem:** Carnitas is +$6 on nachos but a $17 burrito. Carne asada is +$10 on nachos but only $1 more than carnitas in a burrito. These inconsistencies confuse the kitchen and the customer.

**Fix:** Standardize protein tiers across the menu:
- Chicken: +$5 everywhere
- Carnitas: +$6 everywhere
- Carne Asada: +$8 everywhere
- Pastrami: +$8 everywhere

## 4. MENU GAPS (Missing Revenue Opportunities)

### 4A. No Fish or Seafood

This is the single biggest gap. You're in Carlsbad — 1 mile from the ocean. Every competitor has some form of fish. You need at minimum:

- **Fish tacos** (mahi or cod, baja-style) — add as a taco protein option. Cost: ~$3.50/portion. Price: $18 for 3 tacos.
- **Fish & chips basket** — classic sports bar item, easy to execute. Price: $18-19.

### 4B. No Chicken Sandwich

You have a chicken BLT+ ($19) but no dedicated fried or grilled chicken sandwich. This is the #2 selling handheld in the sports bar segment nationally (behind burgers). Draft Republic has a Nashville Hot Chicken Sandwich.

**Add:** Nashville Hot Chicken Sandwich or a Crispy Chicken Sandwich. Price: $18-19.

### 4C. No Quesadilla

With your existing tortilla and protein inventory, a quesadilla is zero new SKUs and pure margin. Price: $15-17 with protein.

### 4D. No Soup / Chili Bowl

You have chili as a side ($8) but no standalone chili bowl or soup of the day. A chili bowl at $10-12 is a high-margin lunch item that uses existing inventory.

### 4E. No Cocktail Pitchers

You offer beer pitchers ($30-36) but no cocktail pitchers. Margarita and sangria pitchers are the highest-margin group drink item in the sports bar segment. Add:
- Margarita Pitcher: $45-50
- Sangria Pitcher: $40-45

### 4F. No Non-Alcoholic Cocktails

Growing segment (7% of bar sales nationally in 2025). You have N/A beers (Corona N/A, Athletic Lite) but no mocktails. Add 2-3 at $8-10.

## 5. ITEMS TO CUT OR CONSOLIDATE

### 5A. Too Many Burger Variants

8 named burgers is a lot. That's 8 builds the kitchen has to remember, and customers experience decision paralysis at 7+ options (Hick's Law). The bottom 2-3 sellers are consuming menu space and creating waste.

**Recommendation:** Cut to 5-6. Keep Pasadena (OG classic), Austin (spicy), Tombstone (cowboy/BBQ), Leucadia (smash — local name), Minneapolis (Juicy Lucy — unique). Cut Bethlehem (overlaps with Tombstone), Hollywood (overlaps with Leucadia), and Irwindale (complex, niche flavor).

### 5B. Munchies Needs Tiering

8 items at one flat price ($12) is operationally messy. Mozzarella sticks and jalapeno poppers cost more than corn dogs and fried pickles. Either:
- **Tier:** Small Munchies $10 (corn dogs, fried pickles, pretzel bites) / Large Munchies $14 (mozz sticks, jalapeno poppers, hummus plate)
- Or: Remove hummus plate (doesn't fit the sports bar identity) and corn dogs (low perceived value for adults)

### 5C. Loaded Fries — Too Many Options

6 loaded fry variants at $18 each. That's a lot of prep for one category. Cut to 3-4 best sellers: Carne Asada, Buffalo Chicken, Bacon & Cheese, Chili Cheese. Drop Garlic Parm and Cheesesteak (the Philly already serves this need).

### 5D. Breakfast Plates are Thin

Only 2 plates (Fallbrook avocado toast, Toasty Toast french toast). For a sports bar doing breakfast, you need:
- **Chicken & Waffles** — $17-18. High margin, brunch-crossover, Instagrammable.
- Or a **Breakfast Quesadilla** — uses existing inventory.

### 5E. Salad Section

4 salads is fine, but the Healthy Hero ($17) is a basic house salad for $17 with no protein. That's a tough sell. Either:
- Include grilled chicken at $17
- Or drop to $14 and let people add protein

The Antipasto ($20) is niche for a sports bar. Monitor sales — if it's bottom 10%, cut it.

## 8. BEER PROGRAM NOTES

### What's Working
- 3 "American Heroes" house beers (IPA, Blonde, Amber) from local breweries — great branding
- N/A options (Corona N/A, Athletic Lite) — growing segment
- Pitcher pricing is reasonable ($30-36)

### What's Missing
- **No local Carlsbad brewery representation.** Burgeon Brewing is literally in Carlsbad. Pizza Port started in the Village. Neither is on your menu. This is a miss for locals who will notice.
- **Only 8 draft lines.** Draft Republic has 100+. You can't compete on volume, so compete on curation. Add 2-3 rotating local taps and promote them as "Village Picks" or "Local Heroes."
- **No Mexican lager on draft.** Pacifico is on draft but consider adding Modelo Especial draft — it's the #1 selling beer in America now.
- **Seltzer selection is heavy** (High Noon, Sun Cruiser, Natrl, White Claw = 4 SKUs). Consolidate to 2 — White Claw + one premium (High Noon or Sun Cruiser). Use the freed cooler space for local craft cans.

## 9. COCKTAIL PROGRAM NOTES

### What's Working
- Heroes Old Fashioned, Sam's Spicy Bloody Mary, Bressi Espressi Martini — good signature drinks with local-sounding names
- Wine list is appropriately sized (6 reds, 6 whites)

### What to Change
- **Costal Margarita ($15)** — Fix spelling to "Coastal." Also, coconut + lime is essentially a coconut margarita. Rename to "Coconut Margarita" or "Carlsbad Margarita" to make the flavor clear.
- **Add a Ranch Water** — Tequila, Topo Chico, lime. Costs ~$2 to make, sells for $12-13. Highest-margin cocktail trending in the sports bar segment.
- **Add Paloma** — Tequila + grapefruit soda. Another high-margin, low-labor cocktail that fits coastal SoCal.
- **101 Rum Runner ($13)** — 6 ingredients = slow to make during rush. Monitor ticket times on this one. Consider simplifying or cutting if it backs up the bar on game nights.
