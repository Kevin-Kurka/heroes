# American Heroes & Brew — Growth Optimization & Automation Plan
*Data-driven, June 2026. Built from Search Console, GBP Performance, and review data.*

## The strategy in one sentence
**Known & loved, but undiscovered.** Convert the huge "near me" search demand into visits, turn every visit into a review, and run the whole loop on automation — measured monthly.

## The data that drives this plan
- **Brand is the engine, discovery is the gap.** GSC clicks are dominated by *branded* searches (162 clicks for "american heroes and brew"); generic discovery queries get massive impressions but ~0 clicks: **"food near me" 1,653 impressions / 5 clicks**, "restaurants near me" 535/6, "breakfast near me" 226/0. → a *ranking* problem, not a demand problem.
- **Reputation is the asset; volume is the ceiling.** 4.7★ (best rating) but **lowest review count** vs competitors — the dominant lever for the Maps 3-pack.
- **Engagement is flat.** ~8,983 GBP interactions over 6 months, peaked in March, gently declining.
- **Position 9.8, CTR 3.8%.** Bottom of page 1 for non-brand terms.

## The funnel — what's done, what's next (optimize + automate)

### 1. DISCOVERY — get found for "near me"
- **Finding:** invisible for "food/restaurants/breakfast near me" (thousands of impressions, ~0 clicks) because the profile was a *Sports bar*.
- **Done (2026-06):** GBP primary → **American restaurant** (+food sub-categories); new `/restaurant` `/burgers` `/cheesesteak` landing pages; homepage now "Restaurant & Sports Bar"; full AEO schema (`VideoObject`/`Event`/`FAQPage`/`Restaurant`), `llms.txt`, vectorized FAQ; **90 legacy WordPress 404s → 301-redirected**.
- **Next:** add **"Hamburger restaurant"** GBP category ("burgers near me" = 121 imp, 1 click); once the **GBP API is approved (~7/2)**, auto-post specials + events to Google.
- **Owner:** earn **"best of" placements** (Patch, Visit Carlsbad, Yelp lists) where competitors appear and Heroes doesn't.

### 2. CLICK — win the click once shown
- **Finding:** position 9.8 / 3.8% CTR.
- **Done:** rich titles + FAQ + Event/Video schema so listings are compelling.
- **Next:** the lever here is **review volume** (raises local-pack rank → higher position) + fresh photos + GBP posts (automated below).

### 3. VISIT / ENGAGE — keep the profile + socials alive
- **Finding:** GBP interactions flat-to-declining.
- **Done (automated):** IG + FB auto-publish (sheet → Apps Script → 2 PM cron, now with a duplicate-post lock); watch-party Story invites (cron); daily-special videos; `/fantasy-football`; live events feed.
- **Next:** Google Posts on the same cadence once the GBP API is live; steady photo additions.

### 4. REVIEW — the #1 growth lever
- **Finding:** best rating, lowest volume.
- **Done:** `/review` one-taps into the Google review composer; on-site Review dialog (one-tap prompts, multi-platform); **NEW printable QR table-tent at `/review/card`**.
- **Next (automate):** monthly **review-count + rating tracker** (email); when GBP API is live, **draft responses** to new reviews; a post-visit review ask (receipt/email/SMS).
- **Owner:** print `/review/card`, place on tables + with the check, and have staff ask — the single highest-ROI habit.

### 5. REPEAT / ADVOCATE
- **Done:** **Hero of the Month** (community tag-to-nominate, homepage card); social presence; daily Reddit community-reply drafts.
- **Next:** capture an email/SMS list for repeat visits; light loyalty.
