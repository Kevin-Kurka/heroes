## 0. The Domination Thesis

We **cannot** win by volume against Draft Republic (1,678 Yelp reviews, 70+ TVs, golf sims). We win by **category dominance + compounding signals**:

1. **Out-niche, don't out-scale.** Own the lanes the giants don't: *family-friendly + weekend breakfast + the only real Philly cheesesteak + every-game on 16 TVs in the walkable Village near LEGOLAND.* Be #1 in a lane you can actually win, then widen.
2. **Win on velocity & freshness, not just totals.** Fresh reviews, fresh content, fresh GBP posts, fresh AI-readable metadata on every asset. Recency is a signal we can dominate immediately — it doesn't require a decade of history.
3. **Compound the flywheel** (Section 2). Reviews → local + AI rank → traffic → more reviews. Content → citations → AI authority → more citations. Each turn makes the next cheaper.
4. **Measure everything, dominate metric-by-metric** (Section 1). What gets a target and a weekly look gets won.

**Current reality (baseline):** best ratings in Carlsbad (4.7★), worst visibility — AI engines rank us *last or absent* across all four clusters because competitors win on review **volume** and **"best of" citations**. The plan attacks exactly those gaps.

## 2. The Flywheel — how the metrics compound

```
  Great experience ──► more & fresher Google REVIEWS (keyworded)
        ▲                                   │
        │                                   ▼
   repeat visits                    higher LOCAL + AI rank
        ▲                                   │
        │                                   ▼
   EMAIL/loyalty ◄── more first-time VISITS ◄── found by humans + AI
        ▲                                   │
        │                                   ▼
   "best of" CITATIONS ◄── PR + UGC + CONTENT ──► AI authority/share-of-voice
```
Two loops, one engine: **(1) Reviews↔Rank↔Traffic** (fast, local) and **(2) Content↔Citations↔AI authority** (compounding, durable). Every dollar/hour goes into a loop, never a dead end. Metadata-stamped content + landing pages feed loop 2; the review engine feeds loop 1; events/email convert traffic to repeat.
