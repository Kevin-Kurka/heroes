## PART 1 — Review-Capture Kit (lever #1: you have the best rating, the worst volume)

**Goal:** 8–12 net-new **Google** reviews/month, with review *text* that plants the phrases AIs echo ("watched the game," "near LEGOLAND," "the cheesesteak," "kids loved it," "weekend breakfast").

### 1A. Staff ask-script (train every server — one ask per shift minimum)
Ask at the moment of a compliment or a clean plate:
> *"So glad you had a good time! If you've got 10 seconds, a quick Google review really helps a local spot like us — just scan this. And if you mention what you came in for — the game, breakfast, the cheesesteak — it helps other people find us for that."*

For **visitors/tourists** (listen for "we're in town / visiting / here for LEGOLAND"):
> *"Welcome to Carlsbad! If you have a sec, a quick review mentioning you visited helps other families find us near LEGOLAND. Means a lot."*

Why the "mention what you came in for" line matters: it's not scripting fake content — it prompts a *real* guest to describe their *real* visit in the keywords AI answer engines read. That's the entire game.

### 1B. Table-tent / register card copy
**Front:**
> **Loved it? Tell the world. 🍻**
> Your quick Google review helps Carlsbad families find us.
> **[QR CODE → americanheroesandbrew.com/review]**
> *Scan • 10 seconds • thank you!*

**Back (optional prompt to seed keywords naturally):**
> Mention what made your visit: *the game on all 16 TVs? Weekend breakfast? The Philly cheesesteak? Bringing the kids?* It helps the next person find us.

### 1C. Placement checklist
- [ ] QR table-tents on every table
- [ ] Small card at the register / by the door
- [ ] QR at the bottom of receipts (if POS allows)
- [ ] One framed "Loved it? Scan to review" sign near the exit
- [ ] **Verify** `americanheroesandbrew.com/review` opens the Google *write-a-review* box on a phone signed into Google (tap-test). If it lands on the listing instead, swap in the exact `g.page/r/...` write link from Business Profile → "Ask for reviews."

### 1D. Response protocol (reply to 100% within 48h — replies are indexed too)
- **5★ no text:** "Thanks [name] — we appreciate you! See you for the next game. 🍻"
- **Detailed praise:** thank + **echo the keyword** ("so glad you loved the cheesesteak and caught the game with us!").
- **Negative:** calm, brief, public, take it offline ("Sorry we missed the mark — we'd love to make it right, please reach us at (760) 994-0187.").

## PART 4 — Community / Reddit Engagement Guide (LLMs weight forums heavily)

**Rule #1: be helpful and disclosed, never spammy.** Most subs allow a transparent local owner who adds genuine value; they ban covert promotion. The owner (disclosed) participates as a real person.

### Where
- **r/sandiego** (large; "where to watch [game]," "North County recs" threads appear constantly)
- **r/Carlsbad** (small but hyper-relevant)
- North County / Carlsbad **Facebook groups**, **Nextdoor** (business page)
- **TripAdvisor Carlsbad forum**

### What to do (legit)
- **Monitor** for keywords: "Carlsbad sports bar," "watch the game," "near LEGOLAND," "family restaurant Carlsbad," "cheesesteak," "breakfast Carlsbad Village." Jump on relevant threads fast with a genuinely helpful answer.
- **Disclose:** "Full disclosure, I own American Heroes & Brew in the Village, but honestly for [their need] we'd be a great fit because [16 TVs / family-friendly / breakfast] — and [name an honest alternative too]." Recommending an alternative builds credibility and reads as authentic.
- Post **real** event announcements where rules allow (game-day specials, World Cup watch, UFC PPV).
- Consider a **disclosed AMA** in r/Carlsbad ("I own the sports bar in the Village, AMA").

### What NOT to do (the bright line — these backfire and are off the table)
- ❌ Fake accounts / sockpuppets praising the bar — mods detect & expose; becomes a negative AIs surface.
- ❌ Covert undisclosed promotion — against sub rules, gets banned.
- ❌ Mass-posting the same blurb — flagged as spam.

### Genuine UGC flywheel
- In-house sign: *"Tag @americanheroesandbrew — game day, breakfast, the Philly."*
- Repost & thank guests who post; light summer prompt ("show us your game-day plate").
- This produces the authentic social/forum mentions AIs trust — at zero cost.
