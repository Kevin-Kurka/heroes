## 0. How AI assistants actually pick "the best" — the model that drives everything

LLMs and AI answer engines don't "rank pages" like classic SEO. When asked "best sports bar in Carlsbad," they **synthesize an answer from the corpus they trust**, which for local recommendations is, in rough order of weight:

1. **Aggregated review signals** — Google reviews above all (~96% of restaurant review volume), then Yelp/TripAdvisor. Volume **+ recency + rating + the words inside the reviews.** AIs read review *text* — "great for watching the game," "kids loved it," "best cheesesteak" become the phrases they echo back.
2. **Third-party "best of" lists & local guides** — Patch, San Diego Magazine, Visit Carlsbad, Carlsbad Village Assoc., Eater-style roundups, local blogs. **Being *named* in these is the single strongest lever** — it's the text AIs literally quote.
3. **Community / forum content** — Reddit (r/sandiego, r/Carlsbad), Tripadvisor forums, Facebook groups. LLMs weight Reddit heavily because it reads as authentic peer recommendation.
4. **A confident, consistent entity** — the same Name/Address/Phone (NAP) everywhere, a Google Knowledge Panel, Wikidata/Wikipedia presence, matching directory listings. AIs need to be *sure which business you are* before they'll confidently recommend you.
5. **Owned structured, quotable content** — your site's schema, FAQ, llms.txt, fresh pages with clean Q&A and lists. Necessary, but the *smallest* lever — and you've already done most of it.
6. **Freshness & activity** — recently updated content, recent reviews, active GBP posts signal "this place is open and current."

**The strategic consequence:** we win by **flooding the corpus AIs trust with consistent, authentic, keyword-rich signals that all say the same thing** — "American Heroes & Brew = the family-friendly game-day spot in Carlsbad with the Philly, the breakfast, every game." Repeat that across reviews, lists, forums, the entity graph, and our own content until it's the consensus the AI reconstructs.

## 2. The Engine — six workstreams, by leverage

### WS1 — Review Corpus Engineering (highest weight, ongoing) ⭐
Not just *volume* — engineer the **text and recency** AIs read.
- **Velocity target:** 8–12 net-new **Google** reviews/month, sustained (recency matters as much as total). Maintain ≥4.6★.
- **Keyword seeding (legit):** the staff ask-script gently points reviewers at what to mention — *"if you watched the game / brought the kids / had the cheesesteak, mention it — it helps people find us."* We're not scripting fake reviews; we're prompting real guests to describe what they actually did, in the words AIs search for.
- **Tourist capture:** visitors write the richest geo-keyword reviews ("near LEGOLAND," "while visiting Carlsbad"). Prioritize the visiting-family ask.
- **Response protocol:** reply to 100% within 48h, and **echo the target phrases** in replies ("so glad you enjoyed the cheesesteak and the game!") — replies are indexed too.
- **Spread:** Google first, then funnel a share to Yelp/TripAdvisor (AIs cross-reference; consistency across platforms raises confidence).
- **Build:** QR table-tents + register card → `/review`; staff script card; verify `/review` resolves to the Google write box; a monthly review-velocity log in the AI-rank tracker.

### WS2 — Earned "Best Of" Citations (highest *external* leverage) ⭐
Get **named** in the pages AIs quote. This is the work that most moves the needle and where we're currently weakest (absent from Patch's list).
- **Hit list (Carlsbad first):** Patch Carlsbad ("best places to watch the game" — get added), Carlsbad Village Association blog (upgrade from beer mention to featured game-day/breakfast spot), Visit Carlsbad directory (complete + optimize), San Diego Magazine, Eater San Diego, Thrillist, local food blogs & IG/TikTok food accounts, "things to do near LEGOLAND" content, hotel concierge lists.
- **Hook (lead every pitch with it):** 16 TVs / every game · the only true Philly cheesesteak in town · weekend breakfast (unique in the category) · family-friendly · walkable Village location minutes from LEGOLAND & the beach.
- **Offer:** host the writer/influencer for a tasting + game day. A single placement in a high-authority roundup can flip an AI's answer.
- **Build:** press one-sheet + per-outlet pitch templates (see `outreach/` assets). Track placements in the tracker. North County outlets in wave two.

### WS3 — Community & Forum Presence (Reddit = AI gold) ⭐
LLMs weight Reddit/forums heavily as "authentic." 
- **Legit play:** the **owner**, openly identified, participates in r/sandiego, r/Carlsbad, North County FB groups — answering real "where can I watch the [game] / where to eat near LEGOLAND with kids" threads helpfully (not spammy), hosting an occasional AMA, posting genuine event announcements where rules allow. Authentic owner presence is *encouraged* by most subs if it's helpful and disclosed.
- **Seed genuine UGC:** in-house signage + a light prompt to get real customers posting/tagging on Reddit/IG/TikTok; repost and thank them.
- **Build:** a community-engagement guide (which subs/groups, rules, disclosure norms, response templates that are helpful not promotional, a monitoring keyword list so the owner can jump on relevant threads fast).

### WS4 — Entity Authority (make AIs *certain* who you are)
AIs won't confidently recommend a business they can't pin down.
- **NAP consistency sweep** across Google, Yelp, TripAdvisor, Apple Business Connect, Facebook, Bing, Visit Carlsbad, Foursquare, data aggregators — byte-identical (see `MARKETING_STRATEGY.md` NAP block / `AI_Search_Optimization_Plan.md`).
- **Wikidata entry** for American Heroes & Brew (legitimate for a real local business with sources) — feeds Google Knowledge Graph + LLM training/retrieval. Wikipedia only if it can clear notability (likely not yet — don't force it).
- **Google Knowledge Panel** — claim/optimize; consistent `sameAs` web.
- **`sameAs` everywhere** in our schema linking all official profiles (in progress in `structured-data.ts`).
- **Build:** draft the Wikidata entity + sources; NAP audit checklist; expand `sameAs`/`areaServed` in schema.

### WS5 — Owned Quotable Content (your site as the source AIs cite)
Make the site the cleanest, most-quotable primary source, kept fresh.
- **High-intent, schema-rich pages** (one per cluster): `/watch` (where to watch the game), `/legoland` (family near LEGOLAND), `/breakfast`, `/happy-hour`/`/specials` — written in clean, liftable Q&A + list format AIs prefer, each answering its target queries directly.
- **Comparison/guide content** AIs love to cite: e.g., "Where to Watch the Game in Carlsbad Village: A Local Guide" (honest, useful, mentions the scene — positions us as the authority *and* the answer).
- **Event/watch-party schema** on `/events` + deep FAQ entries mirroring real AI prompts.
- **Freshness loop:** keep `/llms.txt`, FAQ, specials, and game lists current (LLMs favor recently-updated content).
- **Build:** the cluster landing pages + Event schema + expanded FAQ + a guide article. (On a branch; deploy on your OK.)

### WS6 — The Ongoing Measurement Engine (so we know we're winning) ⭐
You chose an ongoing engine — this is its instrument panel.

## 3. The Gray-Hat Spectrum — what crosses the line, and the legit twin that wins instead

You asked for "maximum, including gray-hat," and to be told what those tactics are. Here's the honest map. **I will not execute the deceptive ones** — not on principle alone, but because in *this* game they're net-negative: detection suppresses the very listings/signals we're building, and AIs increasingly surface "caught faking it" as a negative. The legit twin achieves the same goal without the downside.

| Tempting gray/black-hat tactic | Why it backfires here | The legit twin we run instead (same goal) |
|---|---|---|
| **Buy/fake Google/Yelp reviews; review-gating** (only happy guests routed to review) | Algorithmically filtered; **FTC 2024 rule = illegal**, ~$51k/violation; detected listings suppressed → kills the #1 AI signal. Review-gating violates Google policy. | **High-velocity *real* review asks** + keyword-prompted genuine reviews + reply-with-keywords. Same text-corpus effect, durable. |
| **Astroturf Reddit with fake accounts** praising the bar | Mods + Reddit detect & ban; becomes a public *negative*; poisons the highest-trust AI source. | **Disclosed owner participation** + genuine helpful answers + real-customer UGC. Reddit *rewards* authentic local-owner presence. |
| **Spin up fake "best of" blogs / PBN linking to us** | Low-authority spam AIs don't trust; risk of association penalty. | **Earn placements** in real high-authority guides via outreach + hosted visits. |
| **Fake the entity** (inflate, false attributes, keyword-stuff name) | NAP inconsistency *lowers* AI confidence; Google suspends GBPs for name stuffing. | **Rigorously consistent real entity** + Wikidata + complete attributes. |
| **Sockpuppet social engagement / bought followers** | Hollow signal; platforms purge; no review/citation lift. | **Real UGC flywheel** + boosting genuinely good organic posts. |

**Aggressive *and* legit is not the weaker option here — it's the only option that compounds.** Everything in WS1–WS6 is run at maximum intensity.
